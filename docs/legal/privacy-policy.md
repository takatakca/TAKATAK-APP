## 2. Roles

TAKATAK provides a dashboard to businesses and, under the applicable agreement, may act as a service provider processing information on behalf of a business client. The client remains responsible for the authorizations it grants, the accounts it connects and its own customers’ information that it chooses to import or view.

This policy also covers information about administrators, employees and representatives who use the dashboard directly.

## 3. Information collected

### 3.1 User accounts

Name, email address, role, hashed password or authentication identifier, sign-in logs and preferences.

### 3.2 Client and brand workspaces

Business name, brand, location, authorized users, connection status, configurations and operating notes provided by the client.

### 3.3 Read-only Clover data

When a merchant authorizes the connection:

- merchant or establishment identity and public address;
- inventory categories and items needed for reporting;
- orders and statuses needed for indicators;
- payment totals and details needed for read-only reconciliation.

Access credentials and tokens are stored securely, separated by client/brand workspace and must never be displayed in the dashboard.

### 3.4 Connected social and advertising accounts

If the client enables a related module: authorized Page, profile and ad-account identifiers, posts read through the connection, performance insights, encrypted access tokens and synchronization status. TAKATAK does not collect the client’s social-network password.

### 3.5 Content and support

Text, images, drafts, approvals, comments and files provided by the client; support emails and notes.

### 3.6 Technical data

IP address, browser, device, logs, timestamps, errors, session identifiers and security measurements.

## 4. Purposes

We use information to:

- create and secure separate accounts and workspaces;
- connect the correct Clover establishment to the correct client;
- display reports, indicators and alerts;
- track digital assets authorized by the client;
- prepare drafts and record human approvals;
- synchronize only the modules that are enabled;
- provide support, prevent fraud and investigate incidents;
- improve and correct the service using aggregate or de-identified data where possible;
- comply with law and contracts.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use one client’s data to promote another client.

## 5. AI and automated decisions

Some features may propose text, summaries or recommendations. AI outputs are drafts for human review. We do not make a decision with legal or similarly significant effects based exclusively on automated processing without informing the person and providing the recourse required by law.

Before this policy is published, the actual AI providers, categories of data they receive, locations and retention periods must be confirmed and described in a provider register.

## 6. Disclosure

We may disclose information:

- to authorized users in the same client workspace;
- to Clover or a social/advertising platform under the client’s authorization;
- to hosting, authentication, database, storage, AI, analytics, logging and support providers only to provide the service;
- to professional advisers bound by confidentiality;
- where required or permitted by law.

Each workspace must remain logically separate. Cross-client access is not permitted except for authorized, logged support limited to the need.

## 8. Retention

Accounts and workspace data are retained during the service relationship and afterward according to contractual and legal obligations. Tokens are revoked or deleted when a connection is removed, subject to security logs. Exact retention periods by category **[ARE TO BE CONFIRMED]** in the approved retention schedule.

## 9. Security

Planned safeguards include encryption in transit, encryption or enhanced protection for tokens at rest, role-based access, least privilege, workspace separation, logging, backups and incident-response procedures.

If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec and affected individuals where required and keep the legally required incident record.

## 10. Individual rights

Subject to legal conditions and TAKATAK’s role in the processing, a person may request access, correction, portability, cessation of dissemination or de-indexing, withdraw consent and obtain information about automated processing. If a request concerns data controlled by a business client, we will refer it to that client or direct the person to the client, as appropriate.

Write to takatak.ca@gmail.com. We will respond within the legal period, generally no later than 30 days after receiving a complete request. Complaints may be made to the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

## 11. Cookies and similar technologies

Essential cookies support authentication and security. Non-essential analytics cookies and their purposes will be described in the final version and enabled in accordance with applicable consent requirements.
